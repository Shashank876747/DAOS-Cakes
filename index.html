Our Scratch-Baking Standards & Philosophy

100% Real Whole Ingredients

We bake exclusively with grade-AA sweet cream butter, unbleached flours, cage-free eggs, pure Madagascar bourbon vanilla extracts, and real fruit reductions. Never artificial shortening or premade shelf mixes.

Small-Batch Scratch Craft

Every single sponge layer is freshly weighed, mixed, and baked specifically for your designated event date. We never freeze sponges in bulk or keep stock sitting in freezers for weeks.

Georgia Cottage Food Safety

Operating in rigorous compliance with the Georgia Department of Agriculture Cottage Food regulations. We maintain strict kitchen sanitation, verified ingredient sourcing, and comprehensive food-handling hygiene.

Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

How do I order a custom cake from DAOS Cakes?

You can use our online interactive quote estimator, order request form, or reach out to us via telephone at (470) 476-1631 or email at daoscakes2@gmail.com. We will confirm your design, guest count, flavor profile, and pickup date.

Why is payment strictly in person at pickup?

As an artisanal cottage bakery, we believe in complete transparency and customer satisfaction. You inspect your handcrafted cake in person upon pickup in Smyrna, GA, and complete your cash payment on the spot.

How should I transport my cake safely?

Always place the cake box flat on the level floorboard of your vehicle (preferably the front passenger side floor) with maximum air conditioning running. Never transport a cake on a car seat or in a hot trunk.

How should the cake be served?

While cakes should be kept refrigerated for structural safety, butter-based cakes and Swiss meringue buttercream taste best when brought to room temperature (around 68-72°F) 1 to 2 hours before slicing.
